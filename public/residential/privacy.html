Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Purple Dot Financial Inc. ("we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us, in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy laws.

What we collect

When you submit our enquiry form we collect the information you provide: your name, email, phone number, province, homeownership status, and general details about your property and mortgage. We may also collect standard technical data (e.g. IP address, browser, and pages visited) and advertising identifiers via cookies and pixels (including the Meta Pixel).

How we use it

We use your information to: (1) respond to your enquiry and have a licensed mortgage professional contact you; (2) prepare and discuss mortgage strategy options; (3) improve our marketing and measure ad performance; and (4) comply with legal and regulatory obligations. We contact you only about your enquiry and related services, based on the consent you provide (CASL).

Who we share it with

We share your information only as needed to serve your enquiry — for example, with the licensed underwriter or lender handling your file, and with service providers who help us operate (email, hosting, analytics). We do not sell your personal information. Advertising platforms such as Meta may receive limited event data (e.g. that a lead was submitted) to measure and optimize ads.

Your choices

You can withdraw consent or unsubscribe at any time by replying to any message or emailing us. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.
