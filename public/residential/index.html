Questions

Straight Answers.

Is this actually legal?
Yes. The tax-deductibility of interest on money borrowed to earn investment income is well established under Canadian tax law. The strategies must be implemented correctly to qualify — which is exactly why working with a professional matters. This page is educational and not tax advice; a licensed advisor will confirm what applies to you.
What's the catch — is it risky?
These strategies involve borrowing to invest (leverage), which amplifies both gains and losses. They suit homeowners with stable income and a long-term horizon who are comfortable with market fluctuations. We walk you through the risks honestly before you decide anything.
Do I have to sell my investments or change banks?
Usually not. In most cases we restructure your existing mortgage into a readvanceable one. We'll tell you upfront exactly what would change in your specific situation.
How much does your help cost?
The initial review and strategy consultation are free with no obligation. Mortgage arrangement is compensated the standard way in Canada — we explain all of it transparently before you commit to anything.
What happens after I submit the form?
A licensed mortgage professional personally reviews your information and reaches out — usually within one business day — to discuss your options. No pressure, no obligation.
