Straight Talk

Is This Right For You?

Consolidation is powerful when it's the right fit — and we'll tell you honestly if it isn't.

✅ A strong fit if you…

  • Own a home with roughly 20%+ equity
  • Carry balances on cards, loans, or lines of credit
  • Are paying 15%+ interest on that debt
  • Want lower monthly payments and one due date
  • Have stable employment or business income

Maybe not yet if you…

  • Don't own a home or have very little equity
  • Only have low-interest debt already
  • Are looking to keep borrowing rather than pay down
  • Need a solution that doesn't touch your mortgage

Questions

Straight Answers.

Will this hurt my credit score?
Enquiring does not affect your credit — we start with a soft review and only run a full application with your permission. In fact, many people see their score improve over time once high-interest balances are paid off and utilization drops.
How much could I really save each month?
It depends on your balances, your current rates, and your equity — but replacing 20–29% debt with a mortgage-secured rate typically cuts the monthly cost of that debt substantially. The calculator above gives you a personalized estimate; a licensed advisor confirms the exact number.
Do I have to change banks or sell anything?
Usually not. In most cases we refinance your existing mortgage or add a home-equity line of credit and use it to pay your creditors directly. We'll tell you upfront exactly what would change in your situation.
Is stretching debt over 25 years actually a good idea?
Consolidating lowers your rate and frees monthly cash flow immediately — but a longer amortization can mean more total interest if you only make the minimum. The smart play is to redirect the money you free up back onto the balance. A licensed advisor walks you through the full trade-off honestly before you decide.
What happens after I submit my info?
A licensed mortgage professional personally reviews your numbers and reaches out — usually within one business day — to confirm your exact qualified payment and answer your questions. No pressure, no obligation.
